This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the
date of the first Order Form ("**Effective Date**") by and between **Kocre IT
Services**, located in Stafford, Virginia, USA ("**Kocre IT**," "we," "us"), and
the client identified on the Order Form ("**Client**," "you"). Kocre IT and
Client are each a "Party."

## 1. Definitions
- **Services** — the remote managed IT support and related services described in an Order Form or Statement of Work ("**SOW**").
- **Order Form / SOW** — a document signed (including electronically) by both Parties describing the Services, plan tier, fees, and any project scope. Each Order Form incorporates this Agreement.
- **Plan** — the recurring support tier selected (e.g., Starter, Growth, Scale).
- **Client Data** — data and information Client or its users provide to, or that we access through, the Services.
- **Support Boundaries** — the published scope, ticket definitions, exclusions, and coverage hours referenced in Section 4 and at the Kocre IT "Support & AI Transparency" page.

## 2. Services
2.1 **Scope.** Kocre IT will provide the Services described in the applicable Order Form using commercially reasonable skill and care. Services are **remote-only**; on-site work is not included.
2.2 **Order Forms control.** If an Order Form conflicts with this Agreement, the Order Form controls for that engagement only.
2.3 **Out-of-scope work.** Implementation projects, planned migrations, major remediation, on-site visits, and out-of-hours work are **not** standard Services and are quoted and billed separately via a SOW.
2.4 **Changes.** Either Party may request changes; changes are effective only when documented in a new or amended Order Form/SOW.
2.5 **Subcontractors / subprocessors.** Kocre IT may use subcontractors and third-party providers (including hosting, infrastructure, and AI providers) to deliver the Services and remains responsible for their performance. Processing of personal data is governed by the **DPA**.

## 3. Term & Termination
3.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues while any Order Form is active.
3.2 **Plan term.** After onboarding, recurring Plans are **month-to-month** unless an Order Form states otherwise. Either Party may terminate a Plan for convenience on **30 days'** written notice.
3.3 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate for material breach not cured within **15 days** of written notice (immediately for non-payment beyond the cure period, or for unlawful use).
3.4 **Effect of termination.** Client pays for Services rendered through the termination date. Kocre IT will, on request, return or delete Client Data per the **DPA** and reasonably assist an orderly transition (transition assistance beyond [X] hours is billed at then-current rates).
3.5 **Survival.** Sections 5–13 survive termination.

## 4. Service Levels & Support
4.1 **Coverage.** Standard support is provided during business hours, **Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 ET**, excluding US holidays. Clients in other regions (e.g., UK/Ireland) receive support during the overlapping portion of these hours.
4.2 **Tickets & response.** A "support ticket" and what qualifies as an "emergency" are defined in the Support Boundaries. Kocre IT will use commercially reasonable efforts to respond within published target times. **Response targets are goals, not guarantees**, unless a separate SLA addendum is signed.
4.3 **No guaranteed outcomes.** Kocre IT does not guarantee that systems will be uninterrupted, error-free, or free from security incidents.

## 5. Client Responsibilities
Client will: (a) provide timely access, accurate information, and cooperation; (b) maintain its own appropriate **data backups** unless backup is an expressly contracted Service; (c) ensure it has the right and authority to grant Kocre IT access to its systems and data and that doing so does not violate any law or third-party right; (d) maintain valid licenses for its software; (e) use the Services lawfully; and (f) designate an authorized contact for approvals.

## 6. Fees & Payment
6.1 **Fees.** Recurring fees are billed **monthly in advance**; project fees are billed per the SOW.
6.2 **Payment.** Invoices are due within **[15]** days. Overdue amounts may accrue interest at the lower of **1.5%/month** or the maximum allowed by law, and Kocre IT may suspend Services on **[7]** days' notice for non-payment.
6.3 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of taxes; Client is responsible for applicable taxes (e.g., US sales tax, Canadian GST/HST, UK/EU VAT) other than taxes on Kocre IT's income.
6.4 **Currency.** Fees are stated and payable in **[USD]** unless the Order Form states otherwise.

## 7. Confidentiality
Each Party will protect the other's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform this Agreement, and not disclose it except to personnel/subcontractors with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality. Excludes information that is public, independently developed, or rightfully received without restriction. A Party may disclose as required by law with reasonable notice where permitted.

## 8. Intellectual Property
8.1 **Kocre IT IP.** Kocre IT retains all rights in its software, the client portal, tools, methods, templates, documentation, and know-how, including improvements. Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the portal during the term.
8.2 **Client Data.** As between the Parties, Client owns its Client Data. Client grants Kocre IT a limited license to use Client Data solely to provide the Services.
8.3 **Aggregated/De-identified data.** Kocre IT may use aggregated and de-identified data that does not identify Client or any individual to operate and improve its services.

## 9. Data Protection & Security
9.1 **DPA.** The Parties' data-protection roles and obligations are set out in the **Data Processing Agreement** (`DPA.md`), which is incorporated by reference. For personal data processed on Client's behalf, **Client is the controller and Kocre IT is the processor**.
9.2 **Security.** Kocre IT maintains commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Services (see DPA, Annex 2).

## 10. Warranties & Disclaimers
10.1 Kocre IT warrants it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care.
10.2 **EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS," AND KOCRE IT DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT,** to the maximum extent permitted by law. Kocre IT is not responsible for third-party products, networks, or services, or for issues arising from Client's systems, configurations, or failure to follow recommendations.

## 11. Limitation of Liability
11.1 **Exclusion.** TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE, GOODWILL, OR DATA.
11.2 **Cap.** EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE **FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO KOCRE IT IN THE [12] MONTHS** PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.
11.3 **Carve-outs.** The cap and exclusions do **not** apply to: (a) Client's payment obligations; (b) a Party's breach of confidentiality; (c) indemnification obligations under Section 12; (d) a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; or (e) liability that cannot be limited by law.
11.4 These limitations apply regardless of the theory of liability and even if a remedy fails of its essential purpose.

## 12. Indemnification
12.1 **By Kocre IT.** Kocre IT will defend and indemnify Client against third-party claims that Kocre IT's proprietary tools, as provided, infringe that third party's intellectual property rights.
12.2 **By Client.** Client will defend and indemnify Kocre IT against third-party claims arising from (a) Client Data, (b) Client's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or law, or (c) Client's lack of rights/authority to grant access to its systems or data.
12.3 **Process.** The indemnified Party will give prompt notice, reasonable cooperation, and control of the defense to the indemnifying Party (no settlement imposing obligations on the indemnified Party without consent).

## 13. General
13.1 **Insurance.** Each Party is encouraged to maintain appropriate insurance; Kocre IT maintains/【will maintain】commercially reasonable cyber-liability and errors-and-omissions coverage. *(Confirm/adjust.)*
13.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delays/failures due to events beyond its reasonable control.
13.3 **Independent contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors; this Agreement creates no partnership, agency, or employment relationship.
13.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
13.5 **Governing law & venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **Commonwealth of Virginia, USA**, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules, and the Parties submit to the courts located in **[County], Virginia**. *(For non-US clients, confirm with counsel whether local mandatory consumer/commercial protections apply.)*
13.6 **Dispute resolution.** The Parties will first attempt good-faith resolution. *(Optional: binding arbitration under [AAA] rules — confirm with counsel.)*
13.7 **Notices.** Notices are in writing to the email/address on the Order Form.
13.8 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, the DPA, and Order Forms are the entire agreement and supersede prior understandings. Amendments must be in writing and signed. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. No waiver is implied by delay. Electronic signatures and counterparts are valid.
